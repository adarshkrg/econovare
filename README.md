# EcoNova Resources & Energy Private Limited

Official website for **EcoNova Resources & Energy Private Limited**.

> **Tagline:** Shaping a Cleaner, Greener Tomorrow  
> **Sub-tagline:** World Air Pollution Control and Sustainable Energy Solutions  
> **Core Verticals:** Renewable Energy | Agricultural Products | Marine Resources | Engineering & Technology

## Overview

EcoNova Resources & Energy Private Limited unites renewable energy, agriculture, marine resources, and advanced technology into an integrated sustainability platform, addressing critical climate, energy, and resource challenges through applied research and technology commercialization.

## Core Features & Architecture

- **Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS**
- **Brand System:** EcoNova forest greens, deep teals, olive, mint/aqua waves, and serif headings (`Lora`) with clean sans body (`Lato`).
- **Pages & Routes:**
  - `/` — Homepage (Hero, About, Vision & Mission, Market Problem, Solutions, Offerings, Target Markets, Business Model, Roadmap, Leadership, CTAs)
  - `/about` — About Us, Mandate, Vision, Mission, and Leadership
  - `/offerings` — Core Offerings across Renewable Energy, Engineering, Agriculture, and Marine
  - `/geothermal` — Dedicated Geothermal Heating & Cooling Systems (GHC) technical and economic showcase
  - `/connect` — Contact information, registered Gandhinagar address, and working inquiry form
- **Technical & Scientific Leadership:** Dr. Bijaya Krushna Behera & Ms. Minati Baral
